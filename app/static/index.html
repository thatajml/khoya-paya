How it works

  1. 1

    Describe it, or show it

    Words, a photo, or both. Train and station are optional extras.

  2. 2

    We compare every found item

    An image–text model ranks each record by how closely it fits what you described.

  3. 3

    Claim it at the office

    Mark the one that's yours, then collect it from the lost property office shown, with ID.

Privacy: faces in found-item photos are blurred, location data is stripped, and your search text is never stored, only a hash.
